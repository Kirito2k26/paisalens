BEFORE YOU START

Keep a backup of your records

This tracker saves transactions only in this browser on this device. Closing the tab does not normally erase them.

  • Clearing website data, resetting your browser, or using private/incognito mode can erase your records.
  • Changing your browser, device, or website address will not automatically transfer your transactions.
  • Click ↓ Backup regularly to download a .json backup file containing all your transactions and budgets. Keep it somewhere safe.
  • To move or recover your records, click ↑ Restore and select that downloaded .json file from your device. It is read in your browser and is not uploaded to a server.
  • Restore replaces all existing transactions and budgets in this browser. Back them up first. CSV exports cannot be used with Restore.

Without a saved backup, records erased from browser storage cannot be recovered by this website. There is no automatic cloud backup or device syncing.
