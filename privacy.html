Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how PaisaLens handles information when you visit and use this website.

Overview

PaisaLens is a personal expense tracker that lets you enter transactions, budgets, and spending information in your browser.

Financial information you enter

Your income, expenses, transaction descriptions, budgets, and related records are intended to stay in the local storage of the browser and device you use. The site does not require a PaisaLens login or intentionally upload these records to an owner-managed database. Anyone with access to that browser profile may be able to view locally saved records.

Backups and data loss

If you use the backup or export features, a copy is saved where you choose. You are responsible for protecting that file. Clearing browser data, using a different browser or device, or private browsing may result in records being unavailable or lost.

Advertising and cookies

PaisaLens includes a Google AdSense script. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and—where permitted—personalization. See Google’s advertising technologies information. Any legally required consent mechanism must be configured separately; this policy alone does not provide consent.

Who operates this website

This website is maintained as part of the QELZAVRYN collection. For questions about this policy or the website, email contact.qelzavryn@gmail.com.

Hosting and technical information

When you visit the website, hosting and network providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, requested pages, and access timestamps to deliver the site, maintain security, and diagnose issues. This is separate from information you deliberately enter into the website.

Contacting us

If you send us an email, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond to your message and manage correspondence. Please do not send passwords, bank details, or other sensitive information by email.

Your choices

You can control cookies and locally stored site data in your browser settings. Clearing site data may also remove saved progress or records. You may contact us to ask about information you have emailed us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the website or the services it uses change. The revised version will be posted on this page.
